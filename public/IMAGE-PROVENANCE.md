# StreetFix seed image provenance

All photographs are representative licensed photographs of real places. StreetFix uses them to illustrate fictional reports in a fictional neighborhood. The fixture descriptions, dates, map positions, public updates, and repair outcomes are fictional. The completed-sidewalk image is an illustrative completion fixture, not an actual before/after photograph of any other fixture.

The downloaded files are 960-pixel Wikimedia thumbnails. The app may display crops using CSS. No generative AI images are included. Credits and license links must remain accessible in the prototype and documentation. ShareAlike images and any adaptations of them retain their listed ShareAlike licenses; this does not change the license of unrelated application code.
